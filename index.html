Foundational decision

What kind of association should Bumi Asri become?

This choice determines how contributions, voting, enforcement and vacant plots can be governed.

Path A

Democratic association

Elected representatives with binding authority, subject to an agreed constitution and applicable law.

  • Clear mandate across all plots
  • Formal voting and contributions
  • Higher legal setup burden
Path B · phased start

Voluntary organization

Build participation and trust first, then transfer responsibilities as authority is established.

  • Faster to begin
  • Relies on voluntary compliance
  • Roadmap needed for transition
